# ad hoc formations were standardised into a column organisation, with each column comprising six centuries of 100 men divided into a three sections of 30 men plus a HQ.
# At 600 men a column is a similar size to a full strength infantry battalion, and at 100 men a century is comparable to a weak infantry company.
# Standard: 1 Columna ~ 6 centurias (600 men). Although some adquired extra men on the way to the front, reaching 2000 men. Columna Durruti reached 6k men.
# Some columns had less than 6 centuries.
