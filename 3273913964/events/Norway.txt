#All the parties have 5 different behaviours - supportive, positive, against, opposing and neutral
#Supportive: Will always support the proposal, the player will receive an event where the party acclaims their support
#Positive: Is positive to the proposal, but must gain their support (somewhat cheaper) through negotiations
#Neutral: Is neither positive nor negative to the proposal, and it is needed to negotiate with the party to gain their support
#Against as the non-governing party and not in the coalition: It is possible to gain their support, but it is considerably more expensive to do so
#Against when in coalition but not governing: Will not give their support initially, however will have to negotiate to gain their support (TBD)
#Against when governing party: Will start to experience unrest within the party, event xx7/xx8/xx9
#Opposing: Will never support the proposal, even if they are the governing party or in the coalition
#All parties will have a secret timer (aka event) in which they only can get support from the same party each 120th day
